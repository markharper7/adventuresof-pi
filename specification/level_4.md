# Level 4 Aim

Polygons of increasing sides bounding a circle. 

This one will likely require some maths and some demonstration rather than the user being able to just 'play'

## Level 4 Method

Archimedes' method — inscribed/circumscribed polygons with increasing sides bounding a circle.

## Level 4 Requirements
1. This one will likely require some maths and some demonstration rather than the user being able to just 'play'
2. Demo the maths by showing how you can split a polygon into triangles. Split these in half to get right angled triangles and then knowing the right angle and the radius, you can work out the length of the straight line approximating the section of the circle edge
3. Increase polygon sides to improve accuracy. The user involvement could be selecting the number of sides and then inputting the numbers into a formula shown in the demonstration. Then the drawing is updated to visualise this. 
4. The user can stop at any point after trying a polygon with at least 10 sides. They can carry on for as long as they want. Is there a reasonable maximum that can be drawn? If so let's cap it at something. Maybe 100 sides?
