# Level 6 Aim

Count lattice points in a circle to approximate pi

## Level 6 Method

Gauss circle problem

## Level 6 Requirements
1. Emphasise that the estimation only works if the distance between lattice points is 1. You can use an example of a square to illustrate the point.
2. Minimum of 3 different sized circles to show how the estimate changes. This will unlock the next level. 
3. Ability to play around with many more different sized circles if they want to - likely constrained by the size of the grid for the level
4. Is there a way to make this interactive in some way? Could the first 3 circles be small enough that they are interactive and pi could drop lattice points where required? Then the larger ones are dropped for him and just demonstrations. 
5. A count should be kept in the right hand menu 
6. For the small circles, the user could be encouraged to count the lattice points themselves and then calculate the area using this. The circles would have to be of radius 1, 2 and 3. 4 requires 49 drops which is too many and will make the game no longer fun.
7. Lattice points should be placed on the tile centres. This is already how the game works and it makes for cleaner maths
8. The lattice points could be beacons which flash a colour
9. Let pi drop beacons anywhere but once outside the orbit glow red and aren't counted. This way the player learns by doing. Ones inside the orbit glow green. The first circle could show you this as you go whereas the second and third could get checked once you submit the count and only then does the NPC reveal the correct and wrong ones.
10. If we want to do larger circles that wouldn't fit on a screen, pi could use a telescope to look at different circles where beacons have already been dropped and counted.
