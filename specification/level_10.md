# Level 10 Aim

Nilakantha series. A faster converging series than Leibniz. Race the two against each other.

## Level 10 Method

Nilakantha series: pi = 3 + 4/(2*3*4) - 4/(4*5*6) + 4/(6*7*8) - ...

## Level 10 Requirements
1. Reuse the fraction summing mechanics from level 2 (wood / water or the space equivalent)
2. Two fires / stars side by side. One is fed the Leibniz series, the other the Nilakantha series. Possibly the Leibniz one is fed automatically while the player feeds Nilakantha
3. Show both running values of pi so the player can see Nilakantha reach 3.14 within a few terms while Leibniz is still wobbling
4. Note the series starts at 3 rather than 0. Perhaps the fire / star already starts at a size of 3
5. Maximum number of terms - maybe 6 to 8. It converges quickly
6. Values go to the tray as in other levels
